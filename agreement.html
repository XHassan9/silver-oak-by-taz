1. Parties

This agreement is between TAZ (The Hassan Group LLC, 401 S Graham Street, Charlotte, NC 28202) ("TAZ") and Silver Oak Financial, Pittsburgh, PA ("Client"), signed by Client's authorized partners below. Work starts on Friday, November 20, 2026 (the "Start Date").

2. What's included

  • Silver Oak Financial by TAZ portal for both partners: the CRM (contacts, companies, meeting log, follow-ups and case notes), Prospect Finder, Call Desk, outreach dashboard and Ask TAZ.
  • Verified lead lists to get Client in front of the right people.
  • Setup done for Client: data brought in, accounts connected and the portal ready for both partners, working directly with Aidan on setup and implementation.
  • Small fixes are included. Anything custom is quoted first, and nothing custom is billed without Client's OK.
  • Discounted pricing on future features as they are added.

4. Term

The term is 6 months from the Start Date, with the option to renew. The monthly fee is billed for each month of the term.

5. Client's part

Client supplies its contacts and data, connects its LinkedIn accounts so the dashboard shows real activity, and names who handles compliance for Client. Client is responsible for its own regulatory and compliance review of any outreach sent in its name.

6. Ownership and confidentiality

Client owns its data: contacts, clients, notes and anything it enters. TAZ keeps ownership of its underlying code, templates, portal software and methods, and grants Client a license to use them for Silver Oak Financial during the term. Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential. The portal and its code may not be copied or shared outside Client's business.

7. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid for work completed are not refundable, and amounts owed for work delivered up to the end date are still due. On request at the end, TAZ will export Client's data to Client.

8. Limits

TAZ will do the work professionally and on time but does not guarantee a specific number of leads, meetings or new clients. TAZ's total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees Client has paid in the prior 6 months.

9. General

This agreement is governed by the laws of North Carolina. It is the full agreement between the parties and replaces earlier proposals. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both parties.
